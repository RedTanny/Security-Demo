# SpaceX Latest News — November 2026

## FCC Greenlights SpaceX's 1 Million "Starmind" AI Satellites
On November 25, 2026, the U.S. Federal Communications Commission (FCC) approved SpaceX's application to deploy up to **1 million orbital data-center satellites**—a project dubbed **Starmind**—designed to power space-based AI computing.

- **Manufacturing:** SpaceX plans to build the Starmind satellites at its facilities in Bastrop, Texas.
- **Timeline:** Under the FCC order, SpaceX must place its first operational unit in orbit by November 2033, reach 50% deployment by November 2038, and complete the constellation by November 2040.

## Starship Flight 15 Pulls Off First-Ever Ship Catch
Earlier this month, **Starship Flight 15** (Ship 42) launched from Starbase, Texas on November 13, 2026, deployed 26 Starlink satellites, and completed 14 orbits before returning to Earth. On November 14, **Mechazilla's chopstick arms caught Ship 42 mid-air** at Pad 2—marking the first successful orbital ship catch in the program's history.
